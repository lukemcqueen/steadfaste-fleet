4. RESOLVED — the contradiction was real, and the DOCTOR was right
=============================================================================

A transition symlink was laid at `<HOME>/.hermes-cortex -> <HOME>/.steadfaste-fleet`
as an intermediate step (the migration design's documented behaviour). That exposed a
direct contradiction between two committed checks:

  - `verify-rename-local.sh` REQUIRED the transition symlink, and
  - `stale_premigration_home.py` WARNed on any remaining old path INCLUDING a symlink
    (`tests/test_fleet_doctor_stale_old_home.py::test_stale_symlink_warns` asserts it).

Both cannot be right as written. The operator ruled: **remove the symlinks if we can.**
So the doctor's position won, and it is the correct one — a transition is a transition;
it retires when nothing reads the old name.
